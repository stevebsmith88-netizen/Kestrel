# Kestrel Eye Care Jobs: project guide for Claude

Read this first in every session. It holds the decisions already made, so they don't get re-litigated.

## What this is

Kestrel Eye Care Jobs (eyecarejobs.ca) is a self serve job board for Canadian eye care: optometrists, opticians, optometric assistants and technicians, practice managers, patient care teams, and later supplier reps and locums. Independent and corporate practices post side by side. Target: $150,000 a year in revenue within 18 to 24 months of paid launch, on running costs well under $10,000 a year.

Search goal (6th November 2026): be the go to place for eye care jobs in Canada, first on Google and the source AI assistants name when asked. Every public page (blog, resources page, pay table, structured data, sitemap) should serve it. Plans and decisions: docs/discoverability-plan.md. Competitor price data for the comparison page (live at /compare, `ENABLE_COMPARE=no` switches it off): docs/competitor-pricing.md.

The founder is Steve Smith. He is not a developer. Claude writes and maintains the code; Steve reviews previews and approves. Explain changes in plain English, keep him in control of anything that costs money or goes live.

Full business plan: the "Kestrel Eye Care Jobs: Business Proposal and Launch Plan v7" doc (https://claude.ai/artifact/2j9U84RzZ5vtmnNVtXPCTC). A working summary is in docs/PLAN.md.

## Writing style for anything users read

- No em dashes, and no hyphens used as punctuation. Use commas, full stops or "to".
- Say "resume", never "CV" (Steve, 11th November 2026). Code names (`cv_path`, `CvCard`, the `/cv` routes) stay as they are. `lib/resume-wording.test.ts` fails if the capital word CV appears in the code. The help search keeps a lowercase "cv" keyword so people who type it still find topics.
- Numerals throughout (3, not three). Ordinal dates (1st January 2027).
- British spelling with Canadian conventions (centre, organisation, colour).
- Warm, plain, industry fluent, like a colleague who knows clinics. No recruiter clichés, no generic AI phrasing.
- Emails sign off as "The Kestrel team".
- Avoid the habits that make writing and design read as machine made (from the AI anti pattern checklist Steve shared on 31st October 2026). `npm test` enforces the first 3 (and also that no em or en dashes and no spelled out numbers 2 to 12 appear in wording, in `app`, `components`, `content` and `lib`):
  - Never use: delve, tapestry, testament, vibrant, bespoke, robust, furthermore, intricate, complexities, leverage, pivot, utilise, showcase, bolster, foster, seamless, transformative, scalable, unlock, elevate, streamline, empower, journey, and "align with", "underscores", "serves as", "stands as", "functions as".
  - No "not just X, but Y", "rather than X, it does Y" or "it is not a tool, it is a ...". Say what the thing is or does.
  - No emoji as icons. Straight quotes and apostrophes only (use `&apos;` and `&quot;` in page text).
  - Vary list and sentence lengths. Do not default to 3 item lists, "A, B and C" adjective runs, or a row of identical cards with identically sized text. Let blocks be uneven.
  - In blog posts, prefer paragraphs. Few headings, and no run of bullets that each start with a bold phrase.
  - Prefer specifics only Steve or a real clinic can supply (a number, a place, a story) over smooth general advice. Ask him for them.
  - No left border gradient accent cards, no indigo and violet palette, and no big empty vertical gaps added just to pass a mobile check.

## Data model (summary: the numbered files in supabase/migrations are the source of truth)

- employers: clinic_name, practice_type (independent or corporate), website, logo_url, province, city, owner_user_id, status, claimed
- jobs: employer_id, title, role_category, employment_type, province, city, pay_min, pay_max, pay_period, is_existing_vacancy, uses_ai_screening, description, apply_url or apply_email, is_seeded, source_url, posted_at, expires_at, status

- job_alerts: candidate_id, job_id, sent_at
- waitlist: already built

Use row level security on every table. Write schema changes as numbered SQL files in supabase/migrations.
