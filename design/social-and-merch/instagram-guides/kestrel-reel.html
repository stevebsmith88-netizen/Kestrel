4 things about the bird we're named after.

The kestrel

Kestrels may see ultraviolet light.

Studies of kestrels in Europe found they can spot the trails voles leave, which reflect ultraviolet light.

1 of 4

It can hover in 1 spot.

Facing into the wind, it holds still in the air while it watches the grass below.

2 of 4

The smallest falcon in North America.

Canada's kestrel is 22 to 31 cm long, smaller than a pigeon, and nests across most of Canada in summer.

3 of 4

You can tell males and females apart.

Males have slate blue wings. Females have chestnut wings with dark bars.
