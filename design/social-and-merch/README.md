# Social posts and merch: Things heard in clinic

Drafted 13th November 2026. Designs only: nothing here is used by the website.

Live, editable canvas (private to Steve): https://claude.ai/artifact/YMXrxLJGGjSqQUMVLavQVk

## What is in this folder

- `instagram/`: 8 posts, 1080 x 1350 (4:5 portrait), exported at 2x (2160 x 2700) so they stay sharp in Canva. Post them in number order.
- `stickers/`: 4 sticker sheets (Things we say, Inspired by the kit, My superpower is, Kestrel and eyecarejobs.ca with the mug wrap idea). These are review sheets, not print files. Print files come once the first 8 to 10 are chosen.
- `source/`: the design files behind the canvas (`.dc.html`). Images in them point to the canvas's copies of the brand files in `public/brand` (`kestrel-k-mark.svg`, `kestrel-k-mark-reversed.svg`, `kestrel-wordmark.svg`).

## Brand rules used

Kestrel palette and fonts (DM Serif Display, Poppins, Source Sans 3). The logo, wordmark and K are the files in `public/brand`, never redrawn. Jokes are about the work, never about patients. No lens or frame brand names. Independent and corporate treated the same.

## Instagram: the series and draft captions

The series is called "Things heard in clinic". Every caption ends with a question to invite comments.

| No. | File | Line | Second line | Draft caption |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `01-better-1-or-2.png` | Better 1... or 2? | Both. Neither. Can I see 1 again? | The oldest question in eye care. Optometrists, how many times today? |
| 2 | `02-sorry-about-the-puff.png` | Sorry about the puff. | Said all day. Meant every time. | To every patient who has jumped back from the tonometer: we're sorry. Every single time. Pretesters, what's your record for "sorry" in one shift? |
| 3 | `03-just-look-at-the-house.png` | Just look at the house. | It will go blurry. That's fine. | If you know, you know. What did your first autorefractor show you, a house, a balloon or a barn? |
