## 1. Your advice, and what I plan to do

### 1.1 What happens to a candidate's resume and application

**What happens today.** When a candidate applies, the clinic sees the application as it was sent: the cover note, the resume, and 8 details (name, email, phone, role, provinces where they say they are registered, years of experience, when they are available, and the resume file name). If a clinic deletes its account, its jobs and every application it received are deleted, and they disappear from the candidates' own lists. If a candidate deletes their account, their applications disappear from the clinic's view. The candidate can tick "let this clinic keep my details for future roles", which is saved with the application.

**What I plan to do.**

1. When a clinic deletes its account or a job, the candidate keeps their own record of the application: the job title, clinic name, place, the dates, the last status, and a copy of what they sent. The clinic loses access.
2. Write into the clinic terms and the privacy policy what a clinic may do with an application: use it only to consider the person for that job; keep it for future roles only if the person ticked that box, and then for 12 months unless the person renews it; delete it when the person asks; never share it or add it to a mailing list.
3. Set and publish how long Kestrel keeps each kind of record, and delete on that schedule.

**Questions.**

- **Q1.** Is the plan in points 1 and 2 right? Should a clinic sign a separate data clause (for example a short schedule to the clinic terms), or is a section in the terms enough?
- **Q2.** Once a clinic has opened an application, who is responsible for the candidate's information: Kestrel, the clinic, or both? Who answers a candidate who asks for their information or asks for it to be deleted?
- **Q3.** When a candidate deletes their account, should the clinic keep its copy of applications already sent for a period (for example because an Ontario employer must keep records of its hiring), or should it disappear at once?
- **Q4.** Retention periods. My proposal, for you to correct: unconfirmed waitlist sign ups 30 days; applications and the resume sent with them 2 years after the job ends; "keep my details for future roles" 12 months unless renewed; reports of a job and claims of a listing 2 years; job posts 3 years; messages in a person's account inbox either 12 months or 3 years (I have not decided between them); a person's private job search preferences until they delete their account.

### 1.2 Reporting fake or fraudulent job posts

**What happens today.** Every job page has a "Report this job" link. Anyone can report a post, giving a reason and, if they choose, their email address. Reports go to an admin queue, and we may take a post down while we look into it. No page explains this.

**What I plan to do.** Add a short "Reporting fake jobs" policy page, linked from every job page, the site footer and the help centre. It would say what counts as a fake or fraudulent post (for example a job that does not exist, a request for money or banking details, or someone posing as a clinic), how to report one, that we aim to look at every report within 2 business days, that we may remove the post and suspend the account, and that we tell the person who reported it what we did if they gave an email address. I will send you the draft.

- **Q5.** What must the policy and the reporting process include to meet Ontario's rules for job boards: content, a response time, records we must keep, and whether we must tell the clinic, the person who reported it or anyone else?
- **Q6.** Does the same duty apply to listings Kestrel adds itself from a clinic's own careers page (switched off for now), and does it change anything that every post is read by a person before it goes live?

### 1.3 Who runs the site, and the liability clause

**What happens today.** The terms name only "Kestrel Eye Care Jobs". The limit on our liability is "the amount you paid us in the 12 months before the problem arose", which is $0 for every candidate and for every clinic during the free launch.

- **Q7.** Until the company is incorporated, who should the terms name as the operator, and what changes once it is?

### 1.4 The waitlist and sign up wording

**What happens today.** The waitlist form and the sign up form both have one tick box: "Yes, email me launch news, job alerts, product updates and offers from Kestrel Eye Care Jobs. I can unsubscribe at any time." The exact wording is saved with each sign up. In practice, job alerts work differently: a candidate sets up an alert on its own page, and it is sent whether or not they ticked this box. The waitlist has only test entries today.

**What I plan to do.** Take "job alerts" out of the tick box, so it covers launch news, product updates and offers only, and say beside the job alert form that alerts are sent because the person asked for them. Keep the old wording on record for anyone who signed up under it.

- **Q9.** Is that the right fix? Please suggest the exact wording for the waitlist box and the sign up box.

## 3. What has changed since the pack

Everything below is built. There are still no real users, no payments and no outreach.

- **Resume reading on our own server** now has firm limits: a PDF is read 1 page at a time, at most 12 pages or 8 seconds; a Word file is refused if its text is over 1 MB. Nothing is sent to any other service and the text is not kept.
- **The 30 day hold on name changes** is now in use. An application keeps the name used when it was sent.
- **A clinic's shortlist is never shown to the candidate**, and this is now enforced by the database itself, not only by the website. The candidate sees "Viewed".
- **Only optometrists can apply for optometrist jobs.** Other candidates can apply only for jobs that match their role or the related roles they chose.
- **A candidate can send at most 30 applications in 24 hours**, to stop automated flooding of clinics.
- **Jobs in Quebec are refused** by the website and by the database.
- **Every email that can be unsubscribed from** now carries a one click unsubscribe, and addresses that bounced or complained are never emailed again.
- **When a clinic marks a role filled**, people at the interview or offer stage are sent the clinic's message rather than being left waiting.
- **A clinic's own message to applicants** is sent as plain text, with a line saying the clinic wrote it.
- **The data download** now includes a person's private preferences, the "where are you in eye care" answer, jobs they hid and the date of their last name change.
- **Sign up** now requires a one time code issued by our own website, so accounts cannot be created around our checks.
- **The prices in the pack are out of date.** Decided on 5th November 2026: $59, $79 or $99 per 30 day post depending on the role, and 2 monthly passes ($199 and $349). Payments are not built.
