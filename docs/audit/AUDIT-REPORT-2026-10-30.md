## 1. For Steve (one page)

**Overall:** You have a well built platform that is 2 to 3 months ahead of the plan. The database's access rules held up in every test, no secret has ever leaked, and pages are fast, accessible and well written. It is not yet ready to invite real clinics and candidates. What stands in the way is less the code than the foundations around it: there are no backups, the legal footing is unfinished (people who sign up by email never agree to the terms, the terms have not had a lawyer, and the company does not exist yet), and there is a short list of sign in and data deletion fixes. Most fixes are hours of Claude's work or minutes in your settings. The critical path to launch is now Gate A, and Gate A is yours.

**What is working well (keep it):** row level security on all 17 tables, tested for 9 kinds of user with no leaks and no way to make yourself an admin. 0 accessibility failures across 29 pages, and every colour pair passes. Google job data is complete and correct, and expired jobs drop out properly. Pages load in under 1 second on a phone. The house style is clean (0 em dashes, 0 American spellings). The code is lean, with 0 unused packages. Running costs are about 13 to 28 percent of your $10,000 a year ceiling.

**Top 5 risks**
1. **No backups.** The Supabase free plan keeps none, and CV files are not in database backups on any plan. A bad day could lose every account, application and consent record for good. (R1)
2. **The legal footing is not finished, and the doors are already open.** Email sign ups agree only to the privacy policy, not the terms. None of the 3 policies has been reviewed by a lawyer. And anyone can already create a clinic account and post, before the company and the Eye Recommend disclosure exist. (R2, R10)
3. **2 ways to misuse our own address at sign in.** A link on eyecarejobs.ca can be made to forward people to a look alike site after they sign in. And if the preview wildcard from the Google setup guide is in Supabase, a stranger could capture a Google sign in. (R3, R4)
4. **Deleting can wipe the other side's records.** A clinic can delete a job that was live, in 3 clicks, and every application to it disappears from candidates' trackers. Deleting an account removes the other party's records and any report evidence about it, which matters for Ontario's 3 year record rule. (R5)
5. **Email will run out, and nobody will know.** Every email, including "confirm your email", shares 1 Resend account. On the free plan the 101st email of the day fails. The daily job has no send budget, failures are reported as "ok", and replies to our emails may go to an address nobody reads. (R7, R8, R12)

**Top 5 quick wins** (small effort, real value)
1. Remove the preview wildcard from Supabase's allowed sign in addresses. You, 10 minutes. (R4)
2. Fix the sign in redirect check, and add "I agree to the terms" to email sign up. Claude, under 1 hour. (R2, R3)
3. Make shared job links show the job, not "Coming soon", and stop robots.txt blocking the For clinics page. Claude, about 2 hours. (R15, R38)
4. Keep candidates on their job through sign up and email confirmation, so Google for Jobs visitors are not lost. Claude, a few hours. (R18)
5. Your settings hour: set the reply address and the hello@ and privacy@ aliases, check the Resend plan, and set an Anthropic spend limit. (R7, R12, R24)

**Do this first:** work through Steve's checks 1 to 12 below this week (about 2 hours; the first 3 matter most). Then let Claude do 1 "before invitations" branch with the 2 Blockers and the High code fixes (about 4 to 5 working days, previewed by you). In parallel, book 1 fixed fee lawyer meeting covering the employment agreement, the disclosure letter, the 3 policies, retention and Quebec, and start incorporation. Those decide your launch date more than any code does.

**A coaching note on sequencing:** the build has outrun the foundations. That was a sensible use of personal time, but each further feature built before real clinics use the site is a guess. I recommend freezing new features (the quiz, more templates, more alert options) and building only what unlocks a gate or produces evidence. That means the fixes, measurement (views and apply clicks), the seeding tool and then Stripe.

**Recommended build order, and why:** build only what unlocks a gate or produces evidence.
1. **The "before invitations" fixes:** the 2 Blockers, then R3, R5, R6, R10 (invite only switch), R11, R13, R15, R18, R20, R26, R27 and R8. This protects people and data, and is about 4 to 5 working days.
2. **Measurement (R17, R41):** free launch is where you collect the proof that sells the paid plan, and numbers not recorded then are lost for good. 1 day.
3. **1 "read this page" step serving both the ad builder link and the seeding tool (R16, R19):** it makes the lead pitch true and unlocks Gate C. 2 to 3 days.
4. **The waitlist invite sender and the For clinics page (R11, R42):** this is what a waitlist clinic sees first. 1 day.
5. **Pricing decisions, then Stripe (R46):** needed for paid launch, not free launch. Building it after the decisions avoids rework. 3 to 5 days.
6. **After paid launch, on evidence:** featured listings, the locum board, guest apply (if the data shows a drop), French (before marketing in Quebec), culture match.

If the disclosure is acknowledged by mid December, a free launch in mid January 2027 is realistic, a month ahead of plan. Avoid launching over the holidays. If the disclosure takes longer, the right response is to wait, not to soft launch.

**Assumptions in the revenue model to test:**
- **Market size.** It is unsourced. The useful figure is job ads a year, not practices; a week of counting gives a first number (Steve's check 19).
- **Penetration.** 47 passes is about 1 percent of practices, which is the easy part. 550 single posts implies roughly 420 paying clinics, about 8 percent of practices, which is the stretch.
- **Pass churn.** This is not modelled, and it is the biggest risk, because clinics tend to cancel once the vacancy is filled.
- **Free to paid conversion.** This is not stated. Measure it from day 1 of paid launch.
- **Fees and discounts.** Card fees are charged on the HST inclusive price, Stripe Billing and Tax add fees, and the annual discount and introductory prices are not in the table. Together they take net below $150,000, so aim for about 49 to 50 passes.
- **Jobs4ECPs cutting its price.** That would remove the price argument, leaving traffic, the ad builder and compliance. Only traffic is hard to copy, and it is not measured yet.
- **HST registration.** Cumulative sales pass $30,000 in about month 10 on the plan's milestones, so registering at incorporation is likely simplest. Ask the accountant.
