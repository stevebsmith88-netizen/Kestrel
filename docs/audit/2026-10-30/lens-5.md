# Lens 5: Accessibility and content
Reach: Rendered and tested 29 public URLs (production build against the fake database) with axe-core 4.14.0 at 390px and 1280px, screenshots at 390, 1280, 320 (reflow), 200 percent text and 200 percent zoom, Playwright keyboard runs, contrast maths for every colour pair in app/globals.css, and a full house style search. Signed in pages (Post a job, apply, profiles, applicant board, admin) could not be rendered here, so their forms were reviewed from code only; Turnstile and real screen readers were not available.

## Findings
### 5.1 Required fields are not announced, and the asterisk is never explained
- Severity: Medium  | Confidence: Confirmed
- For Steve: Someone using a screen reader is not told which boxes they must fill in on the waitlist, the clinic profile, Post a job or Claim your listing, and nobody is told what the red star means. It makes forms harder to finish for blind and low vision users, and it is a WCAG AA gap.
- Evidence: components/Field.tsx:8 and :35 draw the star as `<span className="req" aria-hidden="true">*</span>` and never pass `required` or `aria-required` to the box; components/WaitlistForm.tsx:104, 122, 134, 147, 174, 186 do the same. Playwright on `/`: 0 elements with `required` or `aria-required` inside the waitlist form. No page says "* means required" (grep of app/ and components/). The sign up and sign in forms take the opposite approach: `required` on the inputs (components/AuthForms.tsx:72, 111 to 121) but no visible marker, and the "I agree to the privacy policy" box, which is required (lib/auth-actions.ts:43), has neither.
- Detail: WCAG 3.3.2 Labels or Instructions and 1.3.1 Info and Relationships. Because `aria-hidden` hides the star, assistive technology gets no signal at all. The same gap applies to the radio groups built with `Choices` (Field.tsx:26 to 47), which have no `aria-required`.
- Proposed fix: In Field.tsx add `"aria-required": required || undefined` to the cloned control; add `aria-required="true"` to required radio groups; add 1 line at the top of each form, "Fields marked * are required"; mark the required agree boxes the same way. | Effort: 1 to 2 hours | Who: Claude

### 5.2 After a submit with a mistake, keyboard focus drops to the top of the page and some errors do not name the field
- Severity: Medium  | Confidence: Confirmed
- For Steve: If a keyboard or screen reader user presses Submit and something is wrong, the page throws them back to the very top and they have to find the problem field themselves. On the long Post a job form (not yet tested by you) this will be a real struggle.
- Evidence: Playwright ($S/lens-5/flow2.js) on `/sign-up`, `/forgot-password` and `/jobs/1111.../report`, pressing the submit button with empty fields: after the reply, `document.activeElement` is BODY every time, and the sign up form has 0 fields marked `aria-invalid`. Cause: the submit buttons are `disabled={pending}` (AuthForms.tsx:142, JobForm.tsx:174, ApplyForm.tsx:106, ReportForm.tsx:189 and others), and a focused button that becomes disabled loses focus; components/useServerForm.ts and the `useAuthForm` hook have no "focus the first error" step. Waitlist (`/`, empty submit): 5 `role="alert"` messages fire at once, and 2 of them are the identical "Choose an option." (lib/waitlist.ts:73 and :79, for "I am" and "Province or territory"), so a screen reader user hears the same words twice with no field name. The "I am" radios have no `aria-describedby` (checked in the browser), and `Choices` (Field.tsx:42 to 43) gives its hint and error no `id`, so they are not linked to the group.
- Detail: WCAG 3.3.1 Error Identification and 2.4.3 Focus Order. The waitlist form does move focus to the first error on the browser side check (WaitlistForm.tsx:53), which is the right pattern; the server checked forms do not.
- Proposed fix: In useServerForm and useAuthForm, after a reply with errors, focus the first `[aria-invalid=true]` field, or the form error (give it `tabIndex={-1}`); keep the button focusable while sending (`aria-disabled` plus ignoring the second click) instead of `disabled`; give Choices error and hint ids, and put `aria-describedby` and `aria-invalid` on the radio group; name the field in each message ("Choose your province or territory."). | Effort: half a day | Who: Claude

### 5.3 The profile progress bar never shows, and 2 colour names in the stylesheet do not exist
- Severity: Low  | Confidence: Confirmed
- For Steve: The "how complete is your profile" bar on the candidate home page is invisible, because its style was never written. Screen reader users still hear the percentage; sighted users see a gap.
- Evidence: app/candidate/page.tsx:36 and components/FitQuiz.tsx:59 use `className="meter"`, but there is no `.meter` rule in app/globals.css (a script listing every class used in app/ and components/ against the stylesheet found only `meter` and `jobmain` missing; `git log -S".meter{" -- app/globals.css` returns nothing, so it was never added). app/globals.css:192 (`.seedlabel`) and :244 (`.step`) use `var(--slate)`, which is not defined in `:root` (globals.css:4 defines `--muted:#5A6672`, the Slate colour, under another name), so those labels fall back to navy. `.kicker` is only styled inside cards (globals.css:30), so the small "Admin", "Report this job", "Easy Apply" and "The Kestrel blog" labels above page headings render as plain paragraphs.
- Detail: No contrast harm (the fallback is navy), but the design does not match the approved screens.
- Proposed fix: Add `.meter{height:8px;border-radius:4px;background:var(--mist);overflow:hidden;margin:10px 0}.meter i{display:block;height:100%;background:var(--navy)}`; add `--slate:#5A6672` to `:root` (or use `--muted`); make `.kicker` a global rule. | Effort: 20 minutes | Who: Claude

### 5.4 Small keyboard, link and page title polish (grouped, 8 items)
- Severity: Low  | Confidence: Confirmed (items a to g), Likely (item h)
- For Steve: None of these blocks anyone, but together they make the site a little rougher for keyboard, screen reader and zoomed in users.
- Evidence and detail:
  a) The phone Menu stays open after Escape and after tabbing away from it (Playwright on `/jobs` at 390px: `open` is still true after Escape and after 12 Tab presses). components/SiteHeader.tsx:42 and components/AppBar.tsx use a bare `<details>`.
  b) 9 links open a new tab with no warning: app/jobs/[id]/page.tsx:73 (Apply on the clinic's website), AuthForms.tsx:136 and :219, ClinicProfileForm.tsx:46 (2), ClaimForm.tsx:50 (3), ApplyForm.tsx:75. Several sit inside a checkbox label, so a tap on the link text can also tick the box (ApplyForm.tsx:75 "My saved letters").
  c) The 404 page is titled "Kestrel Eye Care Jobs | Coming soon" (app/not-found.tsx has no metadata, so it inherits app/layout.tsx:14). `/find-your-fit`, which is switched off and returns 404, keeps the title "Find your fit in eye care" because the page exports fixed `metadata` (app/find-your-fit/page.tsx:9) and only calls `notFound()` inside the page (line 17).
  d) At 200 percent text size the "Skip to main content" link peeks out as a dark navy bar about 18px tall at the top of every page (screenshots $S/lens-5/shots/*-text200.png; 6,182 navy pixels in the top strip, 0 at normal size). Cause: `.skip{top:-60px}` (globals.css:291) is less than the link's height once text is doubled.
  e) At 390px with 200 percent text, the search box placeholder and the filter boxes on `/jobs` are cut off ("Role, keyword or cit", "Any type of emplo", "On site or field bas"); see $S/lens-5/shots/crop-jobs-390-text200.png. The labels are still read correctly by screen readers, but sighted users lose the words.
  f) Every result on `/jobs` has a button called "View job" (app/jobs/page.tsx:110), so a screen reader's list of links reads "View job, View job, View job".
  g) On `/jobs/<id>/report` the page heading is just the job title ("Optician"); "Report this job" is an unstyled paragraph above it (app/jobs/[id]/report/page.tsx:24 to 25).
  h) The cover letter word counter is a live region that changes on every key press (components/ApplyForm.tsx:58, `aria-live="polite"`), so screen readers will keep saying "57 words, 58 words" while the person types. StatusForm's help text is not linked to its message box (components/StatusForm.tsx:30).
- Proposed fix: close the menu on Escape and on focus leaving (a few lines of script); add "(opens in a new tab)" as hidden text, or drop `target="_blank"` on the policy links; give not-found.tsx its own `metadata` ("Page not found"), and switch find-your-fit to a `generateMetadata` that checks the feature flag; change `.skip` to `transform:translateY(-120%)`; let the 2 search rows wrap or shorten the option text; add `aria-label={"View job: " + j.title}`; make the report page `<h1>Report this job</h1>` with the job title below; announce the word count only on blur or every 25 words; link the StatusForm help with `aria-describedby`. | Effort: 2 to 3 hours | Who: Claude

### 5.5 House style: very clean, 11 small slips (spelled out numbers and 1 Americanism)
- Severity: Low  | Confidence: Confirmed
- For Steve: The writing across the site and the emails follows your style almost perfectly. There are 11 tiny slips to tidy in 1 pass.
- Evidence (searched app/, components/, lib/, content/posts.ts and docs/email-templates/ with Unicode aware grep; commands in the Detail line):
  - Spelled out numbers that should be numerals (9): app/clinic/jobs/new/page.tsx:37 "answer the two questions again"; components/JobForm.tsx:120 heading "Two quick questions"; components/JobForm.tsx:163 "The two questions above are not kept"; lib/auth-actions.ts:93 "The two passwords don't match."; content/posts.ts:11 "A good job ad does two things."; content/posts.ts:75 "stronger than five adjectives"; content/posts.ts:93 "one or 2 sentences" (mixed in the same phrase); app/privacy/page.tsx:151 and components/CvCard.tsx:33 "a link that works for one minute".
  - Americanism (1): lib/waitlist.ts:80 "Check the box to continue." Everywhere else says "tick" (AuthForms.tsx:102, CandidateProfileForm.tsx:29, lib/candidate-actions.ts:121, lib/claim-actions.ts:57). It is an error message, not the consent wording that is saved with each sign up, so changing it is safe.
  - Date format (1, admin only): lib/admin-stats.ts:37 labels the weekly chart "5 Oct" rather than an ordinal date.
- Detail: 0 em dashes or en dashes (`grep -rnP "\x{2014}|\x{2013}"`), 0 hyphens used as punctuation (every " - " hit is arithmetic), 0 American spellings (centre, colour, organisation, licence, authorised all correct; `-ize` search found only code identifiers), 0 recruiter cliches or AI stock phrases in user copy (the only hits are the ad builder's own ban list in lib/ad-builder.ts:24 and the blog post that warns against "fast paced environment"), all public dates use the ordinal `longDate` helper (lib/jobs.ts:272), and every email goes through lib/email-layout.ts, which signs off "Warm regards, The Kestrel team" (line 26 and 33), as do the 3 Supabase templates in docs/email-templates/. "Canada-wide" (app/page.tsx:21) is the approved exception.
- Proposed fix: Change the 11 strings listed. | Effort: 20 minutes | Who: Claude

### 5.6 Sign up by email asks people to agree to the privacy policy only, while Google sign up asks for the privacy policy and the terms
- Severity: Low  | Confidence: Confirmed
- For Steve: People who join with an email and password never tick to accept your Terms of use, but people who join with Google do. Worth making the 2 the same before launch; whether candidates must accept the Terms is a question for your lawyer (lens 4 covers the legal side).
- Evidence: components/AuthForms.tsx:136 "I agree to the privacy policy." and lib/auth-actions.ts:43 "Please agree to the privacy policy to continue."; compare components/AuthForms.tsx:219 "I agree to the privacy policy and the terms of use." and lib/auth-actions.ts:152. Clinics do accept the Terms later, in ClinicProfileForm.tsx:46 and ClaimForm.tsx:50; candidates who sign up by email never do.
- Proposed fix: Use the same sentence on both paths ("I agree to the privacy policy and the terms of use."), once the lawyer has settled the Terms. | Effort: 15 minutes | Who: Claude, after Lawyer

## Good things worth keeping
- axe-core 4.14.0 (WCAG 2.0, 2.1 and 2.2 A and AA rules plus best practice) found 0 violations across 58 runs: 29 public URLs at 390px and 1280px, including `/jobs` with filters, the 3 job pages, report, claim, apply, sign in, sign up, forgot password, blog and 3 posts, contact, privacy, terms, posting policy, clinics, goodbye, waitlist confirm, unsubscribe and a 404 ($S/lens-5/axe-results.json). The only "needs review" items (44 nodes) are the select boxes, because of the arrow drawn as a background image; their text is navy on white, 14.06:1.
- No horizontal scrolling on any tested page at 320px (WCAG 1.4.10 reflow), at 390px and 1280px with root text at 200 percent (method: `html{font-size:200%}` injected, which is what a browser text size setting does, since fonts use rem), or at 200 percent browser zoom (1280px simulated as a 640px viewport with deviceScaleFactor 2). Screenshots in $S/lens-5/shots/.
- Keyboard: a working "Skip to main content" link is the first stop on every page and moves focus to `<main id="main" tabIndex={-1}>` (34 page files have it directly, the other 9 use components/AuthShell.tsx, which has it); tab order on `/`, `/jobs` and `/sign-up` follows the visual order; no focus traps (Tab cycles back to the top, Shift+Tab works); every stop shows a 3px navy outline with a 2px white gap (globals.css:289), which also shows on the navy footer.
- Contrast (computed with the WCAG formula, $S/lens-5/contrast.py): all text pairs pass AA. Body navy on Cream 12.51:1, Soft Navy on Cream 9.44:1, Slate (`--muted`) help text on white 5.87, on Cream 5.22, on Paper 5.54; Chestnut links on Cream 4.96 and on white 5.58; white on Chestnut buttons 5.58 and on Deep Chestnut hover 7.31; error red 6.54; every status badge 5.51 to 10.83; footer text on navy 9.12 and 7.00; input borders (#7B868E) 3.72:1 against white for WCAG 1.4.11. Below 3:1 but not required by WCAG: Wing Buff bullet dots 1.94 (decorative), ghost button and Menu outlines #CDD5DA 1.49 (the button text identifies them), the pending "Sending" button at 70 percent opacity 3.12 (a disabled control is exempt).
- Labels: every visible input on every rendered page has a programmatic label; radio groups use `role="radiogroup"` with `aria-labelledby`; the honeypots are `aria-hidden` and `tabIndex={-1}`; the password eye button is 44px with `aria-pressed` and a changing name; the Turnstile box is a named group ("Security check").
- Autocomplete is right everywhere it matters: given-name, family-name, email, organization, tel, current-password on sign in and delete account, new-password on sign up and reset (AuthForms.tsx, WaitlistForm.tsx, ClinicProfileForm.tsx, CandidateProfileForm.tsx, DeleteAccountForm.tsx). Paste is never blocked (WCAG 3.3.8).
- Targets: header links, Menu, buttons and the password toggle are at least 44px tall (globals.css:68, 70, 72, 75, 104, 108, 234); axe's 2.5.8 target size rule passed on every page.
- `lang="en-CA"` on every page and every email; every public page has a specific title; 1 `h1` per page with no skipped levels (axe heading-order); landmarks header, nav (named "Main" and "Main menu"), main, aside and footer; the job search form is `role="search"` and its result count is a `role="status"` region; the application tracker marks the current step with `aria-current="step"`.
- Motion: no animation, video or carousels; `prefers-reduced-motion` turns off smooth scrolling and transitions (globals.css:135).
- Emails: a single shared layout (lib/email-layout.ts) with `lang`, presentation tables, alt text on the logo, 16px body text and a plain text version, and the house sign off.
- House style is followed almost perfectly (see 5.5), and the ad builder prompt itself tells the AI to follow it (lib/ad-builder.ts:23 to 24).
