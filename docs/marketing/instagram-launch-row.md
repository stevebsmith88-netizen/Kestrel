# Instagram: the launch row

Agreed by Steve on 13th November 2026. A row of 3 Cream posts on launch day, with the middle one a Reel. Nothing here is posted or built yet. Build in Canva once the launch date is set, in the same style as the first 9 (serif headlines, chestnut blocks behind framed photos, the skyline, real stock photos only, the wordmark image wherever the post names Kestrel).

**Before posting, check every claim is still true on the day:** sign up is open, posting is free during the launch, and no card is asked for. Do not quote numbers of jobs or clinics unless they are real on the day.

**Order:** post right, then middle, then left, all on launch day or across launch week, so the row lands whole. Location: Canada. Link in bio: eyecarejobs.ca/links (it switches by itself to Find eye care jobs and Post a job once the site has launched).

## 1st (right): We're open

- **Design:** layout A (framed photo on a chestnut block). Headline "Now open" above the wordmark image, line "Canada-wide eye care jobs. Every job shows its pay.", then eyecarejobs.ca in chestnut, skyline along the bottom.
- **Photo:** a warm, real clinic or optical shop moment (stock, never AI).

Caption:
```text
Kestrel Eye Care Jobs is now open.

A job board built only for eye care in Canada, for optometrists, opticians, optometric assistants, technicians, practice managers and the people at the front desk. Independent clinics and corporate groups post side by side.

Every job shows its pay. Every post is read by a person before it goes live. And there is a "Report this job" link on every job page.

Find your next role, or post your first job: link in bio.

#EyeCareJobs #Optometry #Opticians #OptometricAssistant #CanadaJobs
```

Alt text:
```text
Text reads: Now open, Kestrel. Canada-wide eye care jobs. Every job shows its pay. eyecarejobs.ca. Photo of an eye care clinic, above a drawing of mountains, forest and a city skyline.
```

## 2nd (middle): the Reel, "See how it works"

A short screen recording on a phone, 15 to 20 seconds, with words on screen so it works with the sound off. Record on the live site after launch, with a real job that is happy to be shown (or the demo site, demo.eyecarejobs.ca, with its sample data, saying so in the caption is not needed as long as nothing shown is presented as a real job).

| Seconds | On screen | Words on screen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 to 2 | Cover frame: Cream, serif headline | See how Kestrel works |
| 2 to 6 | Find jobs, choosing a role and a province | Search by role and province |
| 6 to 11 | A job page, scrolling past the pay range and the 2 yes or no answers | Every job shows its pay |
| 11 to 15 | Quick Apply, choosing a resume | Apply in a few taps |
| 15 to 18 | The wordmark on Cream with the skyline | eyecarejobs.ca |

- Quiet music from Instagram's own library, no voice needed.
- **Cover image:** make it in Canva as a Cream post (headline "See how it works", the wordmark, chestnut rule) so it sits in the grid like the others. Keep the words in the middle 3:4 of the frame, since the grid crops it.
- Turn on "Also share to feed" so it appears in the row.
- Use the waitlist or demo account only. Never show a real candidate's name, resume or application.

Caption:
```text
Here is Kestrel in 15 seconds.

Search by role and province. Open a job and see the pay range before anything else. Apply in a few taps with your resume.

No recruiters in the middle and no guessing what a job pays. Link in bio.

#EyeCareJobs #Opticians #Optometry #JobSearch #CanadaJobs
```

Alt text (for the cover image):
```text
Text reads: See how Kestrel works. A short screen recording of searching for eye care jobs by role and province, opening a job that shows its pay range, and applying with a resume.
```
