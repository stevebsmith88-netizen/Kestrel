# Founding clinics: why the offer exists (draft, not published)

Draft of 5th November 2026 for Steve to edit. It is kept off the website until Gate A. Square brackets are things only Steve can fill in. Please check every claim against what is built on the day it is sent.

## The idea in one paragraph

A new board has fewer candidates than one that has run for years, and Google takes months to find and trust new pages. For the first months, clinics should expect fewer applications than they would get from an established board. The founding offer is the fair trade for that: early clinics help build the board, and in return they pay less, for longer, with a direct line to the founder.

## For the website (a short block under the founding line on /clinics)

> **Why founding clinics get this.** Kestrel is new. Candidates find a new board slowly, and search engines need a few months before they send people to new job pages. If you post in the first [6] months or so, you may see fewer applications than on a board that has run for years, and we would rather say so now than have you find out. Founding clinics take that on with us, so they keep their pass price for 12 months, can pay for the year up front at 10 months for the price of 12, and talk to the person who builds Kestrel [by email at any time].

## For the email (one paragraph in the founding invitation)

> A straight answer first. Kestrel is new, and for the first [6] months or so, while search catches up and more candidates find us, your posts may bring fewer applications than a board that has been running for years. Keep posting where you already post. What founding clinics get for helping us start: the pass price held for 12 months, a year up front at 10 months for the price of 12 if you prefer, and a direct line to me [about what is working and what is not].

## Talking points for conversations

- Say the lower response out loud before the clinic asks. It protects trust and avoids a month 2 cancellation.
- Do not promise a number of applications or a date. Say "may" and "the first months".
- Tell them to keep posting on their usual boards in parallel. That is honest advice, and it makes the risk of trying Kestrel small.
- A pass can be cancelled at any time (the Terms say so), and the yearly option is the clinic's choice.
