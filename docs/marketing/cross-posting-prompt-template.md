# Cross posting prompt template (find us post)

Kept for future cross posting only (Steve, 28th November 2026). It was used on 15th and 16th November 2026 for a one off "find us" Instagram post, with the same post written 3 ways: Instagram, Facebook and a Reddit discussion post. To reuse it, change the post idea and the facts, and keep the style rules, which come from `docs/marketing/instagram-plan.md`.

```
I need one extra Instagram post for Kestrel Eye Care Jobs, in the exact style we have already set for the account. [Describe the post here. For the find us post: the 4 places Kestrel lives online, with LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram and Reddit icons at jaunty angles and the caption underneath. No photo.]

Style we have set (please follow it exactly):
- 1 post, 1 image, 1080 x 1350 portrait. No carousel. Keep the content in the middle, since the grid crops towards 3:4.
- Designs are built in Canva with the Kestrel brand kit. Palette: Navy #1B2B4B, Chestnut #A4502C, Deep Chestnut #8C4024, Cream #F7F1E6, Wing Buff #D8B48A, Mist #DCE3E6, Slate #5A6672. Headlines in DM Serif Display, lines in the sans.
- Rows are 1 colour (Cream, Navy or Chestnut). No 2 posts of the same colour family side by side or one above the other. Tell me which colour and which slot in our posting schedule this post fits, without breaking that rule.
- No small labels above a headline. Only use the wordmark image when the post names Kestrel (navy wordmark on Cream, reversed on Navy). Never redraw the logo, the feather or the skyline. The skyline can run along the bottom, used as is.
- Accents we use: a solid block offset 26 pixels down and right behind framed elements (Chestnut on Cream, Wing Buff on Navy, Navy on Chestnut), and a short 90 x 8 pixel rule above a left aligned headline in the accent colour.
- Keep any headline short. Do not use the word "journey".

Give me a Canva build brief (sizes, angles, colours, exact placement) I can follow, and one alternative arrangement.

Caption rules (Kestrel voice): warm, plain, like a colleague who knows clinics. No emoji. Numerals. No em dashes and no hyphens used as punctuation. British spelling with Canadian conventions. Straight quotes. End with "link in bio" for the waitlist, then at most 5 hashtags. Never use the banned words in docs/marketing/brand-voice.md. Also give the alt text, as we do for every post.

Facts to use (check they are still current):
- LinkedIn: Kestrel Eye Care Jobs, https://www.linkedin.com/company/kestrel-eye-care-jobs/
- Facebook: https://www.facebook.com/kestreleye26
- Instagram: @kestreleye26
- Reddit: r/EyeCareJobsCanada, https://www.reddit.com/r/EyeCareJobsCanada/
- The job board opens in early 2027, with the pay shown on every job. Link in bio: https://eyecarejobs.ca/links?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=bio

Please give me 3 versions of the caption, since I will post this on 3 platforms:
1. Instagram, in our usual caption style (links are not clickable there, so point to the link in bio).
2. Facebook, with clickable links and the website link tagged ?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=[campaign name].
3. A text post for the Reddit community, flaired Discussion, with the same links (utm_source=reddit) and ending with a genuine question to the community.
```
