# LinkedIn posts: the first month

**Before posting anything that promotes Kestrel:** the disclosure conversation at Eye Recommend should have happened, and the page must not invite Eye Recommend staff, clinics or contacts, or use anything from Eye Recommend. When to start is Steve's call. The weeks below are relative ("Week 1"), so they can start on any Monday. Each blog post is posted on its Friday, and the link goes in the first comment or at the end, as Steve prefers.

**Rhythm:** Tuesday a question or a short story. Thursday a practical tip. Friday the new blog post. No cold messages. Reply to every comment, and thank anyone who shares.

## Week 2

**Tuesday: question**

An eye care clinic is more than the optometrist. Reception, assistants, opticians, lab technicians, managers, supplier reps.

Which of those jobs do you think people know least about?

**Thursday: tip**

Many people move between eye care roles. An assistant studies to become an optician. A receptionist becomes a clinic manager. An optician moves to the supplier side to train other clinics.

If you are starting out, ask which job lets you learn the most in the next 2 years, not which one you will do forever.

## Week 3

**Tuesday: question**

Interview question for opticians and assistants: what is the one thing you want to know about a clinic before you say yes?

Ours is why the position is open. A new role, a retirement and a replacement are very different stories.

**Thursday: tip**

Ask who chooses the frame collection, and how much say the opticians have. It tells you how much of the job is yours to shape.

## Week 4

**Tuesday: question (engagement post)**

How did you end up in eye care?

Ask a room of opticians, assistants and managers, and the answer is rarely "I always wanted to." It is more often a front desk job, a friend who knew a clinic, or a family business.

We would love to hear yours.

**Thursday: tip**

A short cover letter beats a perfect one. Say why you want this clinic, bring 2 or 3 things you would add, and say when you could start. 100 to 200 words is plenty.

[link: https://eyecarejobs.ca/blog/cover-letter-optical-job]
