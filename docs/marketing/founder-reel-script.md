# Founder Reel: script and shot list

Written 21st November 2026. **Film only after Steve's disclosure conversation at work**: a face to camera video publicly links Steve to Kestrel. Never mention his employer, its members or anything internal. Lines in [square brackets] are for Steve to fill in with his own words and specifics; a real detail beats anything written for him.

## The script (about 30 seconds, about 80 words)

**Hook (first 2 seconds, start talking straight away, no "hi everyone"):**
> Eye care job ads still say "competitive salary". I'm building something to change that.

**Who and why (about 15 seconds):**
> I'm Steve. I've spent [your own line: how long, and in what kind of role, around eye care in Canada], and I keep hearing the same thing from both sides. Clinics can't find the right people, and good people can't tell which jobs are worth applying for.

**What it is (about 10 seconds):**
> So I'm building Kestrel Eye Care Jobs. A job board just for eye care in Canada, where every job shows its pay, a person reads every post, and only people whose role fits can apply.

**Close and a question (about 5 seconds):**
> Pre launch is open now, link in bio. And if you work in eye care, tell me: what would make your next job search easier?

Ending on a question gets comments, and the answers are useful research.

## Shot list

1. **The main shot.** Phone upright, at eye level (prop it on books), chest up, 1 arm's length away. Face a window so the light falls on your face, not behind you. Quiet room, plain or tidy background. Record in the phone's normal camera at 1x, not selfie mode at 0.5x.
2. **3 takes, keep the best.** It doesn't need to be word perfect. Natural beats polished. Look at the lens, not at yourself on the screen.
3. **Optional extra shots (2 to 3 seconds each),** to cut in over the "what it is" part: the eyecarejobs.ca home page on a laptop, your hands typing, the Kestrel feather or logo on screen.

## When posting

- Turn on Instagram's auto captions (most people watch with the sound off) and check the spelling of Kestrel.
- Text on screen for the first 2 seconds: **"Why I'm building Kestrel"**.
- Cover: a frame of you mid sentence, or the cream logo cover (`design/social-and-merch/instagram-guides/kestrel-logo-cover-1080x1920.jpg`).
- Music: none, or very quiet. Your voice is the point.
- Caption:
