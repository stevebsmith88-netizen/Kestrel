## LinkedIn's own "Commitments" section (separate from the rules below)

LinkedIn pages have a Commitments box. It is for promises an employer makes to its employees and the wider community, with relevant links, public reports and accurate credentials as proof. It says not to use it to restate a mission or promote products. Kestrel has no staff, so **leave it empty for now**. If one is added later, the only one Kestrel can prove today is pay transparency, linking to the public pay table (`/resources#pay`) and, once the lawyer has cleared it and the draft banner is gone, the posting policy. Do not add diversity, sustainability or accessibility commitments without something public to point to.

## Posting rules (Kestrel's own commitments to itself, not the LinkedIn box)

1. **1 post a week, at most.** Skipping a week is fine. A quiet page is better than one that posts filler.
2. **Posts come from things Kestrel owns:** a new blog post, the pay table, the resources page, the hiring rules, and later the landing pages. Nothing is invented, and every number has a source on the site.
3. **Steve approves each batch** before it goes out. Claude drafts a batch of 4 posts at a time. Nothing is scheduled by an automatic tool until a month of clean posts.
4. **No unclaimed found listings.** A job found on a clinic's own site is not posted anywhere until the clinic claims it.
5. **No named clinics or people** without their written yes, and no tagging individuals.
6. **No outreach through LinkedIn** (messages or connection requests to clinics) until Steve confirms the disclosure conversation. The CASL outreach rules apply to LinkedIn messages as well.
7. **No Eye Recommend mentions,** and no posts about other businesses' prices unless it is the dated compare page.
8. **Every link carries the tags** from `docs/utm-guide.md`: `utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=` plus the month and topic.
9. **Reply to comments and messages within 2 business days,** in the same warm, plain voice as the site, signed off as the Kestrel team.
10. **Review on the 1st of each month:** which posts brought visits and sign ups (Admin, Waitlist shows the source), and whether the cadence is still realistic.
