## What it tells us

1. **The niche boards the assistants know:** Jobs4ECPs in every eye care answer, then the CAO board, OJobs, OPClassifieds and the OAC list. Kestrel needs to be on the same kind of page (association lists, comparison pages) to be named. Association requests wait for Gate A.
2. **Associations and schools come up a lot:** provincial association boards, OAC, Waterloo, Montreal and the colleges. They are channels for candidates as well as a route to being named.
3. **New names for the competitor check:** OPClassifieds (Steve looked: an unfiltered list of about 1,200 optician ads with ads from 2024 still showing, so a weak competitor that assistants still name) and the OAC job list (not yet captured, see `docs/marketing/operating-rhythm.md`), plus Jobillico and Knighthunter as general boards.
4. **The pay table matches what an assistant quotes** (Job Bank median). Keep it current each year.
5. **Ontario rules:** ChatGPT says the posting rules apply to employers with 25 or more employees. Verify on ontario.ca and with the lawyer, then publish an accurate page on who the rules apply to (small clinics may be exempt).

## What Copilot adds

1. **Copilot never named Jobs4ECPs, OJobs, OPClassifieds or Eluta,** which ChatGPT and Gemini did. Each assistant draws on different sources. Copilot's answers lean on pages Bing has indexed and can quote: opto.ca, Indeed search pages, Job Bank, ontario.ca and law firm articles. To be named, Kestrel's pages have to be in Bing's index and say something an assistant can quote.
2. **The Ontario rules are now confirmed by 2 assistants and an ontario.ca citation:** they apply to employers with 25 or more employees. Kestrel's own pages do not say this (see the Resources page and `/clinics`). Copilot's answer also mixed an Alberta example into an Ontario answer, so an accurate eye care specific page, with real examples, could be the better source.
3. **The gap is real.** Copilot itself says no comprehensive eye care board covers the whole team.
4. **Pay:** 2 assistants quote the same Job Bank figures. Kestrel's median matches. The bottom of the range differs (see the note in `docs/pay-benchmark.md`).
5. **New names for the association list:** the CCOA Career Centre and HealthCareCAN.
