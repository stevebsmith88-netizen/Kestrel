# Backlinks and community plan

Written 9th November 2026 for Steve. A backlink is a link to Kestrel from another site that chose to put it there. Search engines read these as votes of trust, and a new site with few of them is crawled slowly (see `docs/marketing/search-engines.md`). Nothing in this plan is bought, traded in bulk or faked, because Bing's and Google's guidelines both penalise link schemes and artificial promotion.

## The principle

Earn links with pages worth linking to, then ask the people who would find them useful. Most of this waits for Gate A (Steve's disclosure conversation) and for launch, so the "now" list is short.

## Reddit playbook

Hold anything that names Kestrel until the disclosure conversation has happened and launch is close.

The fuller plan (8 topic pillars, the communities to check, the blog posts each Reddit post leans on, and more drafts) is in `docs/marketing/reddit.md` (12th November 2026). The rules below still apply to it.

1. **Account:** a Kestrel named account, never Steve's personal one, with nothing about Eye Recommend.
2. **Learn first:** find the communities about optometry, opticians and Canadian health jobs. Read each one's rules. Note which allow links to your own site, which have a weekly promotion thread and which require moderator approval.
3. **Help for a few weeks:** answer questions, with no links, so the account has a history.
4. **Ask the moderators first** before any post that mentions Kestrel (message below).
5. **Say who you are** in every post. Disguised promotion is the fastest way to be removed and banned.
6. **Put the value in the post,** with a link at the end only where the rules allow it. Use a tagged link: `?utm_source=reddit&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=2027-pay-table`.
7. **Never:** use extra accounts, ask for upvotes, post the same text in many communities, or reply to your own thread as someone else.
8. **Answer comments** for the next few days. A post you walk away from reads as an ad.

## Measuring

- **Bing Webmaster Tools, Backlinks:** a baseline now, then monthly.
- **Google Search Console, Links:** the same.
- **Tagged links:** Admin, Waitlist shows sign ups by source, so a Reddit or association link shows up by name.
- **Review on the 1st of each month:** new links, where they came from, and whether the page they point to is the right one. A realistic aim for the first 6 months after Gate A is a handful of links from relevant sites. A few good ones beat many weak ones.

## What to avoid

Buying links or "backlink packages", link exchanges at scale, comment spam, fake reviews, extra accounts, and low quality directories. These can get the site demoted or removed from search.

## Order of work

1. **Now (no gate):** take a baseline of Backlinks in Bing and Google. Submit to a few quality directories. Keep the pages above accurate.
2. **After Gate A:** association and school requests, the hiring summary pitch, and read the community rules.
3. **Near launch:** the Reddit moderator messages, then the posts.
