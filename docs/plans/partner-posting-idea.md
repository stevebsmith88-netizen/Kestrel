# Partner posting idea (parked, 17th November 2026)

Status: an idea only. Nothing is built, and nothing starts until Steve says his disclosure conversation has gone well. Clinic outreach and publishing found listings stay off until then.

## The idea

Buying and industry groups (Steve named Eye Recommend, OSI and TOG) have their own job sites for their members. On the group's form, a clinic could tick a box so its job is also posted on Kestrel. This could be a low effort way to seed Kestrel with real jobs, and a clinic that ticks could also give Kestrel consent to contact it.

## Ways it could work, simplest first

1. Pre filled link: the tick box opens Kestrel's post a job form with the details filled in. The clinic signs in or up and submits. Nothing to maintain.
2. Feed: the group publishes a feed of only the ticked jobs and Kestrel reads it on a schedule. Jobs go to the review queue like any other. Needs a small reader on Kestrel's side.
3. Direct post: the group's site sends the job to Kestrel with a partner key. Smoothest, but needs a posting API on Kestrel and the most coordination.

## Rules to keep

- Each group gets the same terms, wording and written exception, listed the same way (as with the buying group blog post). Each group's approval is its own written yes. Record each as a new exception in `CLAUDE.md` under Separation from Eye Recommend.
- The clinic opts in, job by job. Only what is already public in the ad is shared, never applicants or member data.
- The group's form must collect what Kestrel requires: pay range (optometrist percentage pay needs expected earnings in dollars), the existing vacancy answer, the AI screening answer. Quebec jobs refused.
- Review, role band and title checks still run before anything goes live.
- Decide who receives applications (Kestrel or the clinic's own link).
- Consent to reach out is a separate tick box, off by default, with its own wording. CASL does not treat posting on a third party site as consent to be contacted. Save the wording, the date and the source group on the clinic. Outreach must still include Kestrel's name, address and a working unsubscribe.
- The privacy policy and terms name the source of these posts. The paralegal sees the wording first (no lawyer yet).
- No scraping: the group supplies the jobs by agreement.
