# Kestrel plan summary

The full plan is the v7 doc: https://claude.ai/artifact/2j9U84RzZ5vtmnNVtXPCTC. This is the short version for build sessions.

## Roadmap

| Phase | Proposed timing | What happens | Gate to move on |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0. Foundations | November to December 2026 | Disclosure to Eye Recommend, incorporate, bank, domain, Kestrel email, privacy policy, trademark search | Disclosure acknowledged; company and bank set up |
| 1. Build | December 2026 to February 2027 | Accounts, posting with ad builder, payments, search, job pages, alerts, dashboard, admin queue, fit quiz | Every flow tested on a phone; policies published |
| 2. Seed and free launch | February to March 2027 | 50+ seeded roles across 5+ provinces, claim your listing outreach, free posts for 60 days, waitlist invited | 50 live roles; job pages indexed by Google |
| 3. Paid launch | From April 2027 | Introductory pricing, monthly pass, monthly reviews; then locum board, supplier roles, featured listings | |

## Running costs from launch

Vercel Pro US$20 a month, Supabase Pro US$25 a month, Stripe 2.9% plus $0.30 per payment, email and Claude API a few dollars a month. About CA$65 a month in total.
