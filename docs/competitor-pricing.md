# Competitor pricing (for the price comparison page)

Captured by Steve from screenshots on 6th November 2026. Re-check every figure on the live pages before anything is published, and show the date on the page.

## Eluta (https://www.eluta.ca/postjobs)

A Canadian search engine run by MediaCorp Canada. Free "organic" results come from crawling employers' own careers pages. The paid product is a Sponsored Job that sits above the organic results.

| Length | Price (plus tax) |
| --- | --- |
| 30 days | $199 |
| 60 days | $375 |
| 90 days | $550 |

- Eluta's own claims, not checked by us: over 785,000 monthly unique visitors in Canada, over 8 million job searches a month (source given as comScore Media Metrix), 6.9 million unique visitors last year, sponsored jobs get 5 times the clicks of organic jobs, no per click charges, company logo included, performance reporting.
- The ordering form asks for the URL of the job on the company's own website, or lets the employer enter it if it is not posted there. 3 steps, with a time limit to finish the page.
- The employers shown on the page are Apple, Agrium and BC Hydro. None is in eye care.
- Steve is not convinced it works well for eye care. Test it before saying anything about it: search eluta.ca for "optician" and "optometric assistant" in 3 cities, count the results, and note how many are independent clinics.

## OJobs (https://www.ojobs.ca/pricing)

An optical job board. Looks inactive: nothing in its news since 2018, no LinkedIn page, and its Facebook page has not posted since 2022 (Steve's checks, 6th November 2026). On the page, say "no recent activity found" and give the dates, never "dead".

| 3 | $200 ($600) | $245 ($735) |

All slots run 30 days. The site also has an Employer of the Month panel, a classified section, a resumes menu and sidebar adverts.

## Kestrel for comparison (prices before HST, from lib/pricing.ts)

- Single post, 30 days: $59, $79 or $99 by role band.
- Clinic pass $199 a month for up to 5 live posts ($39.80 a post). Clinic pass Plus $349 a month for up to 5 live posts including optometrists ($69.80 a post).

Steve is not changing prices now. This page exists to give the comparison page its facts.

## Rules for the comparison page

- Published prices only, with the date captured and a link to the source page.
- Do not repeat a competitor's own audience claims as fact. If quoted, say "Eluta says".
- Describe what each product is (a sponsored search slot, a niche board, a free association board) so the table is fair. Eluta's product is not a board with applicant tracking.
- Competition Act rules on comparative advertising apply. Add the page to the lawyer pack before it goes live.
- Nothing from Indeed, LinkedIn or Jobs4ECPs is scraped. Prices read by hand from public pages are fine.
