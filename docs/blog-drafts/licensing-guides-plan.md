## Rules for writing them

- Facts only from the regulator, the exam body or the school's own site. Never from Indeed, LinkedIn, Jobs4ECPs or another board, and never from a provincial register.
- Each guide says when it was checked and links every regulator, and tells readers to confirm with the regulator before they apply.
- No fees quoted unless the regulator publishes them, and dated if used.
- House style as always: numerals, no dashes as punctuation, paragraphs over bullets.
