# Women in eye care: census research (14th November 2026)

Source: Statistics Canada, 2021 Census of Population, Table 98-10-0449-01 (occupation unit group by labour force status, education, age and gender), total labour force, all education levels. Pulled through Statistics Canada's web data service on 14th November 2026. Statistics Canada rounds census counts to the nearest 5, so small numbers jump about: do not quote shares for Prince Edward Island or Newfoundland and Labrador optometrists. Credit: "Source: Statistics Canada, 2021 Census". Kestrel publishes no Quebec content, so the post uses Canada totals and other provinces only.

Gender in the census is "Men+" and "Women+" (Statistics Canada adds the small non binary count into these 2 groups to protect privacy). Say so in the post.

The third group includes optometric assistants but also many other health assistants, so it can only be a rough guide to assistants. Say so plainly.

## The story: a generational shift

| Age | Optometrists: women | Opticians: women |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 25 to 34 | 69% (1,215 of 1,765) | 79% (1,975 of 2,495) |
| 35 to 44 | 66% | 78% |
| 45 to 54 | 56% | 71% |
| 55 to 64 | 50% | 56% |
| 65 and over | 14% (60 of 420) | 33% (190 of 570) |

Among optometrists 65 and over, about 1 in 7 is a woman. Among those 25 to 34, more than 2 in 3. As the older group retires, a growing share of practice owners and senior opticians will be women.

## Newer headcount (for context)

Statistics Canada's "Vision health month" page says 6,400 optometrists and 11,700 opticians worked in Canada in 2023 (a different measure from the census, so do not mix the 2 in 1 sentence). Gender split for 2023 is not given there, so the census stays the source for the women and men figures.

## Angles

1. **Data post (first):** "Who works in eye care in Canada": the generational shift, the provincial differences (Ontario opticians are much more evenly split than the Prairies), and what it means for hiring: flexible hours, return after leave, the path from optician to owner.
2. **Men in eye care, inside the data post:** men are about 1 in 5 younger opticians and a smaller share of assisting roles. Practical angle for clinics: if your ads and your team page only ever show 1 kind of person, you may be narrowing the field. Ties to "hire for attitude".
3. **Women in Eye Care series, around International Women's Day (Monday 8th March 2027):** short profiles of real women with their permission, found outside Eye Recommend contacts.
