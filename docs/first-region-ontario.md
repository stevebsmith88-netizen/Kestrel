# First region: Ontario (draft plan, 5th November 2026)

Steve's decision: Ontario, Alberta and British Columbia are the first regions, starting with Toronto and Ontario. This is a working draft for Steve to correct. It uses only what is in the repository. Anything marked [Steve] needs a fact or a choice from him, and anything marked [check] needs a source before it is used on the site.

## Why Ontario first

- It has the most people and the most practices, so the board looks full soonest.
- Steve knows the market, and it is where Kestrel is run from.
- Ontario's rules since 1st January 2026 (pay range, existing vacancy, AI screening, no "Canadian experience") are already built into the post form, so every Ontario post is compliant by default.
- Job Bank's snapshot in `docs/market-sizing.md` showed about 20 to 30 optician postings in Ontario at one moment [check, it is a single snapshot].

## The goal

Gate C asks for 50 live roles across 5 or more provinces. For Ontario alone, the aim is about 25 to 30 live roles that look current and clean, so a candidate searching Ontario finds enough to bother. Alberta and BC follow with about 10 each, which together make up the first 50 (see the audit phases).

## Which roles first

Concentrate on 1 or 2 role types so the board looks full where people look. [Steve: which 2?] Working suggestion, to test against the waitlist:

1. Opticians. The largest group (about 11,700 across Canada) and the most advertised.
2. Optometric assistants and technicians. Hired most often, and the $59 band is the easiest price to say yes to.

Optometrist roles follow, since they sit at the $99 band and Pass Plus.

## Where in Ontario

Order by where the most practices are. [Steve: confirm the order.]

1. Toronto and the GTA (Mississauga, Brampton, Markham, Vaughan, Oakville).
2. Ottawa, Hamilton, London, Kitchener, Waterloo.
3. Everywhere else, which the city and radius search (10, 25 and 50 km) already handles, with towns from about 1,000 people.

## How the first listings arrive

All of this is semi automatic, with Steve approving, and stays off until Gate A.

1. **Found listings** (`/admin/found`): the province search for Ontario, then the reader on each clinic's own careers page. 21 day expiry, labelled "Found on the clinic's website, not yet claimed". Publishing stays off (`ENABLE_SEEDING`).
2. **Claim your listing** email to the clinic, one email plus one follow up, once outreach is allowed. Built, off.
3. **Free launch posts** from clinics on the waitlist. Steve can see the clinic and candidate sign ups by province and role at Admin, Waitlist. [Steve: read the Ontario counts there and tell Claude, so the plan uses real numbers.]
4. **Founding clinics** (`docs/marketing/founding-clinics-draft.md`): ask the first Ontario clinics to be founders.

## Candidates in Ontario

No cold email to individuals. Reach them through job alerts (built), the waitlist, the blog and the pathways guide, and clinics inviting their own applicants. Blog posts aimed at Ontario searches ("optician jobs Toronto") are worth writing once there are jobs to link to.

## Counts to track by hand (public numbers only)

A person reads a number and writes it down, as in the market counts. Never copy ads or lists.

- Ontario optician and optometric assistant postings on Job Bank, weekly.
- The numbers of registrants shown by the regulators of optometrists and opticians in Ontario [check where each publishes them]. Counts only, no names.
- Kestrel's own: Ontario waitlist sign ups, live jobs and applications.
