# Fake jobs policy: versions

Ontario's Employment Standards Act requires a job board to keep a copy of every version of its written policy on fraudulent job posts for 3 years after that version stops being in effect. The policy is the page at `/fake-jobs` (`app/fake-jobs/page.tsx`). Every version is also kept in the repository's history; this list says which version was in force when.

When the policy changes: change the page and its "Last updated" date, add a row here with the commit, and keep the old row. Do not delete a row until 3 years after its "In force until" date.
