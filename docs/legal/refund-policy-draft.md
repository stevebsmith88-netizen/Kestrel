## Refunds

Kestrel sells advertising for jobs and businesses in eye care. When you buy a post, a pass, a feature or a sponsorship, you are paying for your ad to be shown on Kestrel for the time you chose. We work hard to put it in front of the right people, but **we cannot promise how many people apply, the quality of applicants, or that you will hire**.

**Job posts.** Once a post has gone live, it is not refundable. If our team does not approve your post, you are not charged, or we refund you in full. If your post is taken down because of a fault on our side, we will put it back up for the time that was lost, or give you a credit.

**A goodwill repost.** If a post does not work out, tell us. At Kestrel's discretion, we may offer a free repost or a credit for a future post. This is a goodwill gesture, decided case by case, and not a right.

**Passes.** You can cancel a monthly pass at any time. It stays active until the end of the month you have paid for, and there are no part refunds. A yearly pass is not refundable once any post has been published under it. If you have not published a post, contact us within 14 days of paying and we will refund it in full.

**Features and sponsorships** (featured jobs, badges, sponsored articles, supporter listings) are not refundable once they have started. If we cannot deliver what you paid for, we will refund the part we could not deliver.

**Founding supporter rewards.** If we do not deliver a reward by the date we promised, you can ask for your money back, and we will refund it.

**Mistakes.** If you were charged twice, or charged the wrong amount, we will refund the difference.

**Taxes.** Prices are shown before HST, and HST is added at checkout and shown on your receipt. Refunds include the HST you paid on the refunded amount.

**How to ask.** Email hello@eyecarejobs.ca with your receipt or the post's title. We reply within 2 business days, and approved refunds go back to the card or account you paid with.

Nothing in this policy takes away rights you have under consumer protection law where you live.

## Notes for Steve (not part of the policy)

- "No refunds, but a free repost at the founder's discretion" is written as the goodwill repost above. Keep it discretionary in the wording, so it is never read as a promise.
- The 14 day refund on an unused yearly pass is suggested so the yearly option feels safe to try; remove it if you prefer.
- Consumer law: individuals (founding supporters) have extra rights in Ontario and elsewhere, for example to cancel when goods are not delivered within 30 days of the promised date. The last line of the policy keeps those rights intact. Clinics and businesses buying advertising are mostly business buyers.
